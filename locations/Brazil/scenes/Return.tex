Yesca in the final hammock: \\
Cricketquiet, \\
Beetlebreeze \& birdcall \\
Smalltown silence, \\
Ruffled branches' windfall \\
Whispered dogfight \\
Butterflies like biplanes: \\
Particles' positions baring \\
Hidden flowing currents. \\

And all the birds in bin'ry rhythm \\
Perch, suspend, \& perch again \\
Perch \& fly \& \\
Rest \& risk \& rest again \\
Glide, exert \& glide again, \\
Til distant thunder scatters.

My Childe, learn to run a cooler fire. \\
Never trust desires which give way to evermore desire.

In the morning,\\
Walk \& autosong \& \\
Paracosmic play \& pause at glassy pool. \\
There a century of beetles, lay in echoed goldenlock, \\
Below electric bulbs. 

Cris comes out \& kicks his legs in chlorine,
Says: This region's known for \\
Climbing, then corrects my accent: \\
``Meu nome é Cristóbal`` \\
I say, Fair enough but good luck iambs, \\
Other rhymes resisting meter.

``Check the slicker''--headed home now--no palheiros. \\
When I ask for climbing handholds \\
What I get are handles: \\
Crimps \& jugs \& pockets \& rails, \\
In-cuts, slopers; slippy, chaussy, glassy. 

(Then he gives an infamous reports from the interior.)

Remember: Back in Brooklyn, \\
Cris had drunken-staggered \\
(Twas a tipsybuzz at best; \\
This telling's fond of its excess) 

--All this money, what does it buy you? \\
--All are heavy, drugged up the wazoo.

Like Liberman had felt, regress \\
From thatchèd Filipino rooves, a devil's deal.

(For gain'o'grain they banner-bound together, \\
Brewed their bread \& booze \& bested all the others.)

Recall-- \\
(Proleptic analepsis) bite o' \\
Meatbeer breakfast, bar o' \\
Mushroom chocolate-- \\
Parque nauseous, weathered under bower, \\
Dozenmile walk, \\
Sip finnest rums on Thames \\
(All overproof) \& \\
Sideline goofing.

``Some folks are just like that-- \\
Treasured by the Gods \& better \\
Off avoiding effort.'' \\
(Others in the darkness drill, \\
Chisel stone to timeless still.)

And me? I was a \\
A screaming Carioc \\
Til dawn-- \\
The rain, the fortune's sons \\
The blueridged mountains, rolling \\
Rivers, smokehoarse \\
(Matthew'd mimicked Satchmo) \\
Then, hungover, hair of \\
Heronbeast--hysteric naked, \\
Raving nightsweat, bedwet-- \\
``Brotha!''

We were freeks. \\
Labrats in labyrintime. \\
Sufferers of zugunruhe. \\
We were epicormic buds. \\
Damage to the main trunk activated our waiting phototropism.

We sped cuntry to seedy, \\
A classic migration. \\
Cycles turning analepsis to prolepsis, \\
Idylling cars and the echoing birdcalls, \\
A kind of uniformitarianism,\\
Prophet's perfect tense not far from carvèd stone-time:
--Wenn du mich siehst, dann weine. \\

So ontos recaps phylos, sure, \\
But how do you choose your form? \\
How do you choose your name? \\
How do you choose to kick \& rise? \\
How do you choose your game?

So us \& all the yunguns \\
Tempted by the views of light that shine through screens \\
Of distant wildlife, missed encounters, curtained parties \\
In the City of Rooms, \\
In the City of Tunnels, \\
Working our way, amazèd, towards it, \\
Missing the great white light that shines \\
Through keyhole polymorphous, \\
And only wanting, for color, \\
The light they are adjacent. \\
No final form, so what then, might I risk becoming? \\
Dirt, perhaps a paperback, a silver lizard sunning.

At the edge of town our car engulfed in darkness. \\
Distant android ziggurat. \\
Back to the City of metamours, kipple \\
Mordor \& Yoyodyne, fabrictrend ripples.

Where they eat numbers. \\
Where the shiny people dance to shiny music. \\
And gather in the Afters \\
Of the snake 'n ladders of careers, \\
Between bedrock \& birdsong, \\
Lost to time forever.

And me? Voidward, narrowcasting still. \\
A failson, a faildaughter, \\
A paterdreaming pitter-patter.
