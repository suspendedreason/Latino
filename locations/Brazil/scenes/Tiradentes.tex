Ariel--I was sitting on a park bench, Tiradentes, when you texted. \\
Said: ``To feel the cool of rain \& heat of sun at once-- \\
That is a higher pleasure''-- \\
Meant it.

Now this lucky duck gets to see a \\
Sun dog on phanerothymes, \\ 
A god within. \\
Is permitted, even, abbioccio, \\
In breeze-blocks, plastic tables, \\
White square tables, cross from Cris \\
Who reads aloud his Anki.

Or perched among the stoneyflower, \\
Starseed, phototropes while \\
Roots reach down, commensurate \\
With upward motion-- \\
Sky-grasp by a strong foundation-- 

And speaks of fabled Brásil lightness, \\
Burdened less by static contracts-- \\
In support he quotes Calvino; \\
Cards to read in native language.

--It was something like: A relaxation.
