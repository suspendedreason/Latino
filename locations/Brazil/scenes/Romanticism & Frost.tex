Half-savage in an Industrial Age, \\

In florried snow is buried Robert Frost, \\
New England's famèd pasture poet. \\
All a means to butter parsnips.

Whose reading was lowercase catholic; 
Who leafed through anthology; \\
Who sought to know \& move all kinds: \\
Here are the pearls that were his eyes: \\
No caviar! He sought a subtler \\
Quality in tonal tenor, shied from all \\
Performance of his learnéd reference. \\

New leaf; another ten and ten; \\
Wetfingered triple-tap to learn \\
